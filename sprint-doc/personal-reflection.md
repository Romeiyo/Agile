# Sprint 1 — Personal Reflection

## Key Learning
A Sprint allows work to flow more efficiently and it allows for a greater sense of accountability from the team because we know what each person is reponsible for and how much time they have to accomplish it.

## Assumptions That Failed
Estimation broke down the most because we did not clearly define the sub-tasks from each user-story.

## Behavioural Change
Better planning for our sub-tasks according to our acceptance criteria.

## Professional Growth
Proper communication with the rest of the team with clarity and understanding without feeling shame of blame.
